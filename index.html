Field Study No. 14

Pheidole rhea A Colony Survival Simulation

Direct a young colony through its first seasons. Grow your population, store enough to feed the queen, and hold the nest against recurring army ant raids.

How to Play

Objective

Keep the queen fed, grow the colony, and survive the army ant raids that arrive on a rolling timer. Losing the queen ends the study.

Camera

  • WASD Pan the camera when no ant is under direct control.
  • Scroll wheel Zoom in and out.

Direct Control

  • Click any ant or the queen to take direct control of it. The camera follows automatically.
  • ↑↓←→ Move the controlled ant.
  • Space Context action — attack, hunt, dig, place a wall, pick up or drop brood, or feed the queen and larvae, depending what's nearby.
  • Click empty space, or press Release Control, to hand the ant back to automatic behavior.

Tools (Pointer Mode)

  • Pointer — click a dirt wall touching open space to queue a dig job for your workers. Click an ant to control it.
  • Forage Pheromone — mark ground with a scent trail; nearby idle workers are drawn toward it to search for food.
  • Attack Pheromone — mark ground near an intruder; nearby majors and minors are drawn toward it to fight.

Colony Roles

Minor
Fast forager and digger. Cheap to raise.
Major
Stronger fighter and forager. Costs meat.
Super Major
Heavy defender. Can plug a tunnel entrance.
Queen
Lays eggs when fed and not hungry. Protect her.

Feeding & Brood

Workers carry seeds and meat back to the queen automatically, which restores her hunger and banks the resource. Larvae need a single feeding partway through their growth — stand a carrying ant on top of a hungry larva and press Space to feed it.

Strategies

Early Colony

  • Spend early seeds on minors. They forage and dig fastest and cost no meat.
  • Keep an eye on the hunger gauge — a starving queen stops laying eggs and slowly loses health.
  • Let workers stockpile a buffer of both resources before raising a major or super major.

Before a Raid

  • The raid warning gives you a moment to prepare — pull foragers back toward the nest entrance.
  • Take direct control of a super major and use Toggle Plug Mode to seal a tunnel chokepoint against incoming army ants.
  • Drop an attack pheromone near the entrance before ants arrive; nearby defenders will already be moving into position when the raid lands.

During a Raid

  • Army ants go after loose brood first, then whichever ant is nearest. Keep brood deep in the nest, away from the surface.
  • Majors and super majors will break off other tasks to assist any ant under attack — a cluster of them near the entrance holds a line well.
  • Direct-controlling a major yourself lets you focus fire on the army ant doing the most damage.

Long Term

  • Dig a second chamber deeper underground to store brood far from the surface entrance army ants use.
  • Balance your population between foragers and defenders — an all-defense colony starves, an all-forager colony gets overrun.
  • Hunting prey on the surface is the fastest way to bank meat for majors and super majors.
